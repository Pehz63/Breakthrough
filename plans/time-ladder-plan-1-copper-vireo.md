# Plan: what ms is a reasonable bound for the wall-clock track?

Settled interactively with the developer on 2026-09-06, immediately before the
run. Recorded here because the design choices are the part worth arguing with
later, and three of the four were the developer's call rather than a default.

## The question

`ranking/CHAMPION.md` splits the throne across a node-normalized and a
wall-clock-normalized track. The wall-clock track is defined at `time=150ms`,
and that number was never derived from anything. Two facts made it worth
settling properly:

1. **The node track does not normalize compute.** Measured on the existing store,
   the same node budget costs 18x to 36x more per move on `position_elo mlp
   model=113` than on the chip counter. A node budget normalizes nodes, which is
   a legitimate question (strength per node isolates evaluator quality from
   evaluator speed), but it is not a compute comparison.
2. **The wall-clock track was not a wall-clock instrument either.** Pre-fix,
   `time=` overshot by 3.9x to 7.1x (`TIME BUDGET NOT ENFORCED`,
   `Docs/corrections.md`), and only one bound was ever rostered, so there was no
   ladder to read a trend from.

## Design decisions

| decision | choice | why |
|---|---|---|
| criterion | **the knee of Elo against realized ms** | Developer's choice from four offered (knee, where the budget binds, where the two tracks agree on ordering, CPU-matched to the node track). Plain reading of "reasonable": the cheapest bound past which more time stops buying strength. |
| cores | **4, dropping `tdleaf_self lin model=349`** | Developer's call. `model=349` and `model=169` are the same regime and architecture and tracked each other across all 40 cells of the node study, so the fifth cell bought nothing. |
| ladder | **25, 50, 100, 200, 400ms** | Developer's choice over a wider 25-1600ms option. Accepted limitation, stated before the run: `position_elo mlp model=113` costs 252-1431 ms/move in the node grid, so it may still be climbing at the top rung. If it shows no knee, report no knee rather than fitting one. |
| `retain` | **included, plain and retain at every rung** | Doubles the cells. Worth it because `retain` changes realized ms per move, so it changes the answer to the ms question rather than being a separate question. |

**Revised after the first pass, 2026-09-06.** The `retain` row above describes the
first pass only. On the developer's instruction the plain condition is retired and
the ladder extends to 800 and 1600ms, `retain` only: plain realizes 0.432 of its
flag (sd 0.053) against `retain`'s 0.854 (sd 0.028), so a plain `time=` head is
not a wall-clock instrument. Theory 70, and the reasoning is recorded under
"Decision: the plain condition is retired" in the companion results document. The
first pass's plain cells are benched `off`, not deleted.

40 cells. Head `ab(deep=12,tt,ord[,retain],time=Xms)@3` throughout, so every
comparison is within one search head and one core.

**Measure against REALIZED ms, not the flag.** `nextIterationFits` declines an
iteration it predicts will not fit, so a time-budgeted search finishes well short
of its allowance. The flag is not the spend, and a knee located on the flag axis
would be located on the wrong axis.

## Sequencing

Run after the `ab @2 -> @3` bump, not before. The bump discards every stored
`time=` game, and this study replays that territory on the fixed binary anyway,
so bump-then-study costs nothing extra and avoids rating a mix of pre- and
post-fix games.

Generated by `tools/make_time_ladder_roster.py --abver 3`. Pinned against
`ranking/standings.tsv`, which after the bump has no row under 1,000 games and is
therefore safe to pin directly.

## What would make this study wrong

- If the knee sits above 400ms for most cores, the ladder answers nothing and
  needs extending. Predicted for `model=113` specifically.
- If `retain` changes realized ms enough that the plain and retain curves are not
  measured on comparable axes, the two conditions must be compared CPU-matched
  (retain at flag X against plain at flag 2X) rather than rung by rung.
